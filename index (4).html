Connect Studio Desk to Supabase

Your people, channel records, video metrics and warm-up notes will be saved in a hosted database instead of browser storage.

One-time setupCreate a Supabase project, run the included supabase-schema.sql, and add your project URL and publishable key to config.js. See README.md in the download.

No person or account data is stored in localStorage. Only the signed-in session is kept temporarily in sessionStorage.
